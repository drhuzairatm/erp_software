# Staff onboarding and security guide

**For:** every staff member of Dr.Expert Edulinks and GRADUATER.

**Covers:** your account, signing in, keeping it safe, and the security rules for students, documents and payments.

**Role details:** for day-to-day work in your role, see USER_GUIDE.md.

## 1. Your invitation and activation
1. An administrator creates your profile (name, official email, company, role, department) and sends an **invitation**. It arrives by email from the company's sending address, or the administrator gives you the link personally if email is not yet set up.
2. Open the link **yourself**, on your own device. It works **once** and expires (normally after 7 days). If it has expired or says "Invitation not valid", ask the administrator to **resend**; older links stop working.
3. The page greets you by name and shows your email. Create your **private password**:
   - at least 12 characters
   - using three of: lowercase, uppercase, numbers, symbols
   - not used anywhere else

   Press **Activate my account**. This also confirms your email address.
4. Nobody (not the MD, not an administrator) can see your password. Nobody from the company will ever ask for it.

## 2. First sign-in and mandatory two-step login
**Two-step login is compulsory for every staff account.** You cannot use the system until it is set up.

1. Go to the staff portal address. Enter your **work email** and **password**, then **Sign in**.
2. You are taken to **Two-factor login**. Install an authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or similar.
3. Press **Set up two-factor login**. Scan the QR code with the app (or type the key shown under it).
4. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and press **Turn on two-factor login**.
5. **Save your 10 recovery codes now.** They are shown only once:
   - **Store them:** write them on paper kept in a locked place, or in a company-approved password manager.
   - **Never store them:** in email, chat, photos on your phone, or a file on the desktop.
   - **Each code works once.** Use one only if you lose your phone.
6. From now on, every sign-in asks for the current 6-digit code after your password.

**Lost or new phone:**
- **You still have recovery codes:** sign in with your password, enter a **recovery code** instead of the 6-digit code, then set up the app again on the new phone (your name → **Two-factor login**).
- **No codes left:** contact the MD. They will verify your identity (video call with ID, or in person) and reset your two-step login. You then set it up again.
- **Always report a lost phone or laptop at once** (see section 7).

## 3. Password and session safety
- **Change your password:** your name (top right) → **Change password**. Other sessions are signed out.
- **Forgot it:** use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, or ask an administrator for a reset link.
- **Sessions:**
  - Sessions end after 12 hours, or after 8 hours without activity.
  - Lock your screen when you step away.
  - Never share your account; every action is recorded under your name.
- **See where you are signed in:** your name → **Two-factor login** → **Sign-in activity** shows active sessions and recent sign-ins. If something looks wrong, press **Sign out everywhere** and report it.
- **On a shared or public computer:**
  - Sign out when done.
  - Use **Sign out everywhere** afterwards if you are unsure.
  - Do not save the password in the browser.
- **Check the address bar** before entering your password. Never sign in from a link in an unexpected email.

## 4. Students, assignments and tasks
- **What you can see:** only what your role and company allow. Edulinks and GRADUATER records are separate; documentation staff see only students **assigned** to them. If a student you need is missing, ask your manager to assign them. Do not ask colleagues to share screens or files.
- **Tasks** (sidebar → **Tasks**):
  - Open a task to see its student, due date and history.
  - Update the status as you work.
  - If you are waiting on someone outside the company (student, university, embassy), choose that reason. It separates external delays from internal ones.
  - Managers assign and reassign tasks. A reassignment is recorded with the previous owner.
- **Notes** are internal. Write facts, not opinions, and never record passwords or card numbers.

## 5. Documents
- **Upload:** student → **Documents** tab → choose the file → **Upload**. Only PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB. Uploading never approves anything.
- **Review** (documentation staff):
  1. Open the document.
  2. Check each requirement, then **Verify** and **Submit**.
  3. A manager who did not verify it gives the final **approval**.
- **Corrections:** if a manager returns a document with a comment, upload the corrected version. The review starts again, and earlier checks do not count.
- **Virus-scan status:** every file shows one of the following.

  | Status | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | **Virus-scanned: clean** | Checked by the scanner. |
  | **Not virus-scanned** / **Virus scan failed** | Cannot be approved or sent by courier until scanned or manually cleared. |
  | **Manually cleared (not scanned)** | An authorised person released it after checking. This is **not** a virus scan. |
  | **Infected – quarantined** | Blocked everywhere: the viewer, downloads, the student portal, approval and courier all refuse it, even for the MD. Ask the student for a clean copy. |

- **Manual clearance is restricted:** only people the MD has authorised (the MD by default) can give it. Never the person who uploaded the file. A reason is always required, and every clearance is reported to the MD. Do not ask anyone to clear a file to "save time".
- Open documents in the **built-in viewer**. Do not download files unless your work requires it; downloads are recorded, and unusual volumes alert the MD.

## 6. Payments (finance staff)
- **Recording:** record charges and receipts on the student's **Payments** tab, with the correct currency, rate and reference.
- **Verification:** a receipt counts only after **another** finance person verifies it. You cannot verify your own entry.
- **No edits or deletes:**
  - Entries can never be edited or deleted.
  - Mistakes are corrected with a **reversal** (reason required).
  - Rate corrections also need a reason, and the history is kept.
- **Other roles:** the CEO sees counts only, never amounts. Counsellors and documentation staff do not see the ledger.
- **Card details:** never write card numbers, CVV codes or bank passwords anywhere in the system.

## 7. Report anything suspicious, at once
Tell the **MD** (or your manager, who informs the MD) immediately if:
- you see sign-ins or activity you did not do
- you lose a phone or laptop used for work
- a student file looks strange, or your antivirus warns after opening a file
- someone asks for your password, codes or student data by phone, email or chat
- you see student data where it should not be (WhatsApp groups, personal email, outside the company)
- payments look wrong

Do not try to investigate or delete anything yourself. Reporting quickly is never wrong.

## 8. Leaving, suspension and termination
- **Suspension or termination:**
  - When an administrator suspends or terminates your account, you are signed out everywhere immediately and cannot sign in.
  - Your tasks, notes, uploads and history stay in the system for the company's records.
  - Your open tasks are handed to a colleague.
- **Before you leave:** finish or hand over your work, and do not download or keep copies of student data. Downloads and exports are recorded and alerted.
- **Reactivation:** a suspended account can be reactivated by an administrator. A terminated account can only be reinstated by the MD.
